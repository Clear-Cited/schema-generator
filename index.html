How schema helps AI and search

Structured data (JSON-LD) is a small block you add to a page that states its facts explicitly — what your organization is, a product's category and price, or the Q&A on an FAQ page. LLM answers lean on entities ("is Acme a company or a product, and which one?") and on extractable facts (price, category, FAQ answers). JSON-LD states those unambiguously, so ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, and Google AI can identify, trust, and quote you — and it's a long-standing rich-result signal for Google. Frequently asked questions

    
What is JSON-LD structured data?
A small block of machine-readable data you add to a page that states facts about it explicitly — what your organization is, a product's category and price, or the Q&A on an FAQ page. Search engines and AI assistants read it to identify your entity and pull clean facts.

    
How does schema help AI and search?
LLM answers lean on entities and on extractable facts. JSON-LD states those unambiguously, making you easier to identify, trust, and quote in AI answers — and it's a long-standing ranking and rich-result signal for Google.

    
Where do I put the JSON-LD?
Paste it inside a <script type="application/ld+json"> tag in the <head> of the relevant page. Organization goes on your homepage, Product on a product page, FAQ on a page that shows those questions and answers.

    
Which schema type should I use?
Organization to establish your entity (homepage), SoftwareApplication/Product on a tool or product page for category and price, and FAQPage on pages with real buyer questions, since short Q&A pairs are highly extractable by AI.

    
Will structured data alone get me cited?
No. Schema makes you easier to understand and quote, but AI citations also depend on crawler access, an llms.txt, clear content, and third-party mentions. It's one piece of a full AEO and SEO foundation. Learn how AEO works →
